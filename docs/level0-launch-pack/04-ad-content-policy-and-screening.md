# 04 · Ad content policy + screening checklist

**Status:** internal operating document; a five-line summary goes on the public page. This is the file that keeps
Level 0 *legally boring*. Publishing someone's ad copy is a act of publication by you — under the Consumer
Protection Act 2019 (misleading advertisement / unfair trade practice), the IT framework, and the various
state money-circulation and gaming laws, "the advertiser said it" is a weak defence when you took the money and
wrote the row.

**Time:** 4 minutes per listing while you're small. Automate it only when 4 minutes a day becomes an hour.

---

## Part A — Hard refusals (no exceptions, no "we'll fix it later")

| # | Category | Why it is a refusal, not a caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Online money games: betting, casino, rummy/poker-for-money, colour-prediction, fantasy with stakes, "tips" for any of it | Publishing the ad is itself restricted conduct for a platform taking payment for promotion; banks and processors treat it as a rail-kill. Your own memo: advertising money games is an offence in itself. |
| 2 | Crypto/forex/"binary" schemes promising returns, "double your SOL", mining ROI offers, token presales with revenue promises | Securities/forex exposure (SEBI, RBI, FEMA), and it is the single biggest consumer-complaint generator in India |
| 3 | Unregistered investment advice, "guaranteed" returns, stock-tips channels, loan "guaranteed approval" | SEBI (IA) / RBI-licensing territory; you become the front door for the fraud |
| 4 | Health, medical, ayurvedic "cure", weight-loss, mental-health claims; magic-remedy style products | Drugs & Magic Remedies Act + ASCI health claims; a claim you print is a claim you published |
| 5 | Adult content, escort/"matrimony" that is a front for it, any sexualised service | Payment rails, minor-safety, and Indian IT Rules obscenity exposure |
| 6 | Weapons, ammunition, explosives, restricted drugs, tobacco/vaping promotion, alcohol sale promotion | Prohibited or heavily restricted trade |
| 7 | Pyramid / MLM / "money circulation" schemes, referral income as a % of downline, chit-fund style "invest and recruit" | Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act 1978 |
| 8 | Anything whose own site is a phishing clone of a real brand, or a lookalike of another board | Trademark infringement + consumer deception + you'd be selling impersonation |
| 9 | Malware, drive-by download prompts, "browser required", fake update pages, sites with a `Safe Browsing` warning | You'd be the distribution channel |
| 10 | Hate, communal content, harassment tools, doxxing, non-consensual intimate imagery, election manipulation | IT Act + criminal law; not worth one bid |
| 11 | Government impersonation (`.gov.in`-lookalikes, "we are the official portal"), judiciary/police lookalikes | Criminal |
| 12 | Services requiring a licence where you cannot see the licence: money intermediary, deposit-taking, insurance, real-estate RERA projects, pharma e-sale | Ask for the licence number; absent it, refuse |
| 13 | A listing whose only purpose is to attack, mock or impersonate another Advertiser on this board | Turns a directory into a dispute magnet and kills the product |
| 14 | **Anything that involves paying or rewarding visitors** — "join and earn per click", "watch to earn", "stake and win" | That is Level 1/2 territory. At Level 0 you must not host it, because hosting it is how you end up with a money loop you never built the compliance for. |

**Borderline but allowed with edits:** "get more readers", "grow your blog" (puffery, fine); "increase your Google
rankings" (**refuse the wording** — promise of search outcome; ask them to rewrite); "AI-detector-free content"
(allowed); "buy followers" (refuse — platform-terms violation and often bot farms); "guest post packages" with a
link-network smell (allowed, but label as link selling, no SEO-outcome claim).

## Part B — The 10 checks (do all of them, in this order, before you send a payment link)

| # | Check | How, in under 60 seconds | Fail ⇒ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Domain control | Ask for an email at that domain, or a TXT record `branks-verify=<random>` you invent. | Refuse until proven (Terms 2.2) |
| 2 | Domain age & history | WHOIS creation date + `web.archive.org` for the host. A 6-day-old domain promising 50k readers is a red flag. | Ask what it is; refuse if vague |
| 3 | The page is real | Open the URL: not a parked page, not "under construction", not a link-in-bio with zero content. | Reject, offer re-list when live |
| 4 | HTTPS + no Safe Browsing verdict | Click the padlock; if unsure, check the Transparency Report / VirusTotal site scan. | Refuse |
| 5 | Claims scan | Read the homepage for the Part A list and for "guaranteed", "registered SEBI/RBI", "govt approved", "₹ per day", "100% refund". | Refuse, or require rewritten listing copy |
| 6 | Complaint search | Google `"[brand]" scam`, `"[brand]" refund`, `"[brand]" complaint`; check Reddit/X in 2 minutes. | If material and unanswered, refuse |
| 7 | Name clash | IP India TM search for the brand in class 35, plus a plain Google of the exact name + competitor names. | Refuse if it trades on another brand |
| 8 | Category honesty | Does the described thing match the site? (A "developer tool board" that is a betting app.) | Reject the category, not always the listing |
| 9 | Their own policies | Does *their* site have contact + refund/terms if it sells anything? | Require it; you're the front door |
| 10 | Screened by you, not by them | You write/confirm the row text. No HTML, no images they paste unfiltered, no emoji-stuffed "SEO" strings, no phone numbers or emails that turn your board into a scraping target. | Rewrite or refuse |

**Evidence you keep per listing (30 seconds, in the ledger from `06`):** date, the 10 results, a screenshot of the
page as listed (full-page, one file), and the exact row text approved. If a claim ever arrives, that folder is
your defence.

## Part C — After publication

- **Objection from a third party:** acknowledge within 72h; ask for specifics (rights asserted, URL, their
  identity); suspend the row while the claim is live if it is a credible infringement or illegality claim, tell
  the Advertiser, give them 3 business days to respond; decide; restore or remove and record it.
- **Advertiser abuse of you** (threats, fake payment receipts, chargeback-after-delivery): no listing, no
  refund, brief written note of why, and stop corresponding after the second reply.
- **Every rejection gets the same two-sentence template:** what failed, what would change it. Never a lecture,
  never "policy says". Example: *"Didn't accept it — the site promotes returns on a token sale, which we never
  list. If it becomes a plain developer-tool blog post series without the investment promise, send it back and
  I'll look again."*

## Part D — The 5-line public version (paste under "Rules")

> **We say no to:** betting and real-money games, crypto/forex return schemes, investment or loan guarantees,
> medical claims, adult, weapons, pyramid/recruit-to-earn schemes, impersonation of any brand or government,
> malware, hate. **We also say no to** anything that pays visitors to click. Every row we publish was reviewed by a
> human who owns this site — the row is our copy of your one-liner, and you prove you own the domain before it
> goes up.
