## [BRAND] Refund & Cancellation Policy

A listing is advertising placement bought up front. This policy says exactly when we give the money back, so
that nobody has to argue about it later.

### 1. Full refund — automatic, no explanation requested, within 5 business days

1. **We didn't publish it.** 2 business days pass after a cleared payment with no live row and no written reason.
2. **Wrong data.** The row as published differs from what you submitted — name, link, category or the amount
   shown — and we can't fix it to your satisfaction within one business day.
3. **Rejected at screening.** Your site falls under a prohibited category in the Ad Content Policy. If the money
   is already with us, it goes back; nothing is charged for a listing we refuse.
4. **Duplicate or overpayment** caused by our instructions or a payment that failed after you retried.
5. **Your site was taken down by the hosting incident** — a mistake of ours (bad deploy, deleted row, broken
   import) and the row stays down for more than 24 hours.

### 2. Refund pro-rata (the unused part of any defined period)

6. We shut the board down or stop operating the listing service while your paid **period** is still running —
   see clause 10 of the Terms. Perpetual-position slots are delivery-of-placement and are covered by clause 10.1
   of the Terms, not here.

### 3. No refund

7. **"It didn't work."** No clicks, no sales, no rankings, no indexation, no coverage. We never promised an
   outcome (Terms 1.2), so there is nothing to refund for an outcome.
8. **You changed your mind** about the copy, the category or your own pricing after the row has been live for
   `[REFUND-WINDOW]` days.
9. **You got outbid.** Losing the top row is the product working, not the product failing.
10. **You gave us wrong or incomplete information** and the row was published as submitted.
11. **We removed your row for breaching the rules** (prohibited category, impersonation, refusal to prove domain
    control, abusive correspondence). No refund, and no future sales.
12. **Price or rule changes for future bids.** The floor, increment or copy on the site may move; that is not a
    refund event for rows already sold.

### 4. Cancellation

13. You may ask us to **remove** your row at any time. Removal is not cancellation: we don't return money for a
    placement already delivered. We will happily take the row down on request if staying up is worse for you than
    being listed — that costs you nothing and it is one reason people buy again.
14. If you want to *rescind the purchase*, you may do so inside `[REFUND-WINDOW]` days of payment **if we have not
    published the row**. Once it is live, section 3 applies.

### 5. How to ask

Email **`[CONTACT]`** from the address on the order, with the payment reference and one line about what went
wrong. We acknowledge within 72 hours on business days and decide within 5 business days of your message.
Money returns to the **original instrument** — never cash, never a UPI handle that isn't yours, never a
"credit note" in place of a refund you asked for.

### 6. Chargebacks

A chargeback withdraws the listing immediately and we stop selling to that site. We will supply your order form,
the payment record and dated screenshots of the live row to the issuer, and these terms plus the click-through
acceptance are the evidence. If you were going to be unhappy, email us — the refund is easier for both of us than
the dispute.

### 7. This policy is a floor, not a ceiling

If we've wronged you in a way this page doesn't cover, we will do the fair thing and then write the case into this
policy. Nothing here takes away any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 or any other law.
