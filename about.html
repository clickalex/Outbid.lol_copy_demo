The story

A nine-day trend, turned into a reference.

19 Aug 2026

The trend ignites

A solo founder ships outbid.lol — one page, one input, rank set entirely by what you pay. It draws a million visitors and six figures of bids in 48 hours, and the clone wave starts the same week.

The wave

Hundreds of boards, one directory

Copycats launch by the dozen. The public outoutbid.lol directory begins verifying and tracking every one of them — and publishes the whole inventory as open data (CC BY 4.0). That feed is this project's ground truth.

26 Aug 2026

Baseline: the market audit

Report 001 ships — product model, category mix, money concentration, risks — with a bot that re-pulls the directory and re-computes every figure daily. The entry simulator follows the same data.

27 Aug 2026

From report to toolkit

The decision-tools suite, the shippable launch kit and the nine-document Level 0 compliance pack land on top of the research. The site becomes Outbid OS: research in, product out.

Same evening

Re-scan, audit, lock down

Every gap claim re-verified at 450 boards (six new zero-entry verticals added), a 20-pass audit loop fixes five real bugs and hardens the whole repo, and every board count on the ideas page moves under bot control.

How it's maintained

Five rules this project won't break.

Static by design

No framework, no bundler, no server, no login. If a static host can serve a folder, this site works — including from a local disk, offline.

The bot owns numbers, humans own judgements

A daily bot re-pulls the open directory and rewrites every counter, table and chart it owns. Idea verdicts and gap claims are hand-verified and dated — a bot never marks an idea "built".

Evidence over vibes

Every "nobody has built this" claim is a keyword scan over the whole inventory, with the count shown. Every market figure traces back to the committed snapshot.

Scope-locked launch kit

The launch kit ships empty and honest: paid placements disclosed, refunds defined, screening documented — and a guardrails doc listing what Level 0 must never add.

Every change audited

One audit command checks the whole repo — syntax, structure, links, sentinels, data consistency, runtime behaviour. Releases land only after consecutive clean passes.
