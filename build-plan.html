Roadmap

Seven days from idea to kill-or-continue.

A practical build plan for a tiny pay-to-rank experiment. Keep scope tight and decide with paid-bid evidence.

DAY 0Pick one ideaUse the idea picker. Write the one-sentence promise and first 10 bidder names.
DAY 1Static boardBuild the landing page, ranking table, entry card and share screenshot state.
DAY 2PaymentsAdd checkout/manual payment proof and publish only after cleared payment.
DAY 3ModerationWrite rules, takedown flow, refund/dispute terms and blocklist.
DAY 4Seed entriesInvite first bidders personally. Do not rely on a public launch post.
DAY 5LaunchPost one screenshot, one promise and one CTA where the niche already lives.
DAY 7DecideContinue only if week-one kill metric is hit: e.g. 10 bidders or $100 gross.
NEXTImprove or stopIf it works, add analytics and categories. If not, archive and move on.
