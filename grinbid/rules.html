Rules of Grinbid 📜

A low-stakes, free, fan-powered carnival. Be kind, no bots, no real money.

13+ only

Virtual coins only

All coins are 100% free virtual coins with zero cash value. They are not currency, not gambling and cannot be redeemed, exchanged, refunded or sold for anything of real value.

No real-money purchases

There are no purchases, no microtransactions and no pay-to-win mechanics. No payment processor takes money for coins, boosts, ranks or perks. Any payment screen is a voluntary donation.

Boosts and rankings

  1. Boosts are playful virtual expressions of support.
  2. A boost costs 50+ virtual coins and adds 1× points (or 1.5× on your own page).
  3. Because coins are free and non-purchasable, rankings measure fan enthusiasm — never spending power.
  4. Boosts have a 2-second cooldown to keep the feed fair.

Fan-created profiles

Seeded profiles are fan-created tributes. They are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the real people, estates, venues, brands or communities they depict. Real owners can submit a claim; moderators review evidence and mark verified pages with the 🟢 badge.

Fair play and abuse

  • You must be 13 or older.
  • No bots, scripted signups, mass accounts or spam.
  • No self-referrals. Referrals from the same IP as the referrer are flagged pending review.
  • No harassment or fraudulent claim requests.
  • Accounts breaking these rules may be suspended.

Donations

Donations are voluntary contributions to keep Grinbid running. They are strictly non-reward — they never grant coins, boosts, ranks, multipliers or perks.

Changes

Numbers (streaks, drops, prizes) can be tweaked to keep the carnival fun and fair. Grinbid will never introduce real-money purchases for coins or rank advantages.

🪙 Coin reminder: virtual, free, zero cash value, no redemption. This is a game, not a currency.
