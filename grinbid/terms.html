📜 Terms of Grinbid

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · Grinbid is a free fan-made game. Play fair, be kind, and keep tributes respectful.

1. What Grinbid is

Grinbid is a free, fan-made game where users create fan tribute pages for public figures and well-known fictional characters (celebrities, sports stars, actors, musicians, influencers, comic/anime/movie characters, venues, brands and communities), then "boost" those pages with free virtual coins to climb a weekly leaderboard. Every page is created by a fan — there are no official pages, and nothing on Grinbid is affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or connected to the real people, characters, estates, venues or brands depicted.

2. Virtual coins only — zero cash value

Grinbid coins are 100% free virtual coins awarded as a game mechanic (signup bonus, daily claims, lucky drops, tasks, referrals and season prizes). They hold zero cash value, are not currency, are not crypto, are not gambling or a financial product, and cannot be purchased, sold, traded, redeemed, exchanged, refunded or transferred for anything of real value. Rankings measure fan enthusiasm only — never spending power, because nothing can be bought.

3. No real-money purchases · donations are non-reward

There are no purchases, no microtransactions and no pay-to-win mechanics. No payment processor takes money for coins, boosts, ranks, badges or perks. Any donation option (UPI, PayPal, Buy Me a Coffee, Razorpay) is a voluntary contribution to keep the project running; donors receive no coins, boosts, ranks, multipliers or perks of any kind, and donations never influence gameplay.

4. Fan pages are user content — and they are tributes

  • Every fan page is created and submitted by a user. Grinbid does not author them.
  • Pages are unofficial fan tributes only. Do not present a page as official, and do not claim or imply the real person/team endorses it.
  • The 🟢 verified badge means a moderator accepted a claim from someone representing the subject — it verifies the claimant, it is not an endorsement of the page content by Grinbid.
  • Every submitted page is reviewed by an admin/moderator before it goes live. We may reject, unpublish, edit badges on or remove any page, and suspend accounts, at our discretion.

5. Content rules — what you may not create or upload

Keep it fun, fan-first and lawful. When you create a page, upload a photo or write text, you must not:

  • Impersonate — pretend to be the real person, their official team, or Grinbid staff; or falsely claim verification.
  • Target private individuals — pages are for public figures and well-known fictional characters. Don't create pages for ordinary private people without their consent.
  • Post hate, abuse or harassment — no content attacking someone over race, religion, gender, sexuality, nationality, disability or identity; no threats, doxxing, or targeted harassment. Rival fan banter is fine; hate is not.
  • Post sexual, explicit or NSFW content, sexualized depictions of real people, or anything inappropriate for a 13+ audience.
  • Defame or spread falsehoods about real people.
  • Infringe rights — only upload photos/art you own or have permission to use (e.g. your own art/fan art, or images you are licensed to use). Don't upload copyrighted photos, logos or artwork you have no rights to. You are responsible for everything you upload.
  • Spam, advertise or sell — no commercial promotion, scams, links to gambling/real-money sites, or off-site solicitations on pages.
  • Create pages for the purpose of attacking a subject (hate pages) — this is a boost game for fans, not a downvote weapon.

6. Your content stays yours — you license us to show it

You keep ownership of the text and images you submit. By submitting a page (including an uploaded photo), you grant Grinbid a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to host, store, resize, display and distribute that content within the game (for example, we resize uploaded photos in the browser and store them to show on the page). You confirm you have the rights to grant this license. This license ends when the content is removed; cached/archived copies may linger briefly.

7. Moderation, takedowns and rights-holder claims

  • Approval queue: new pages are invisible to the public until an admin approves them. The creator can see their own pending page.
  • Removal: we may remove or reject content that breaks these rules, with or without notice.
  • If you are the real person (or their authorized representative) depicted on a page: use the in-app Claim page action with evidence, or contact the admin, to request verification or removal. We review every claim.
  • If a page uses your photo/art without permission, or breaks any rule above: contact the admin with the page link and details, and we'll review and take it down where appropriate.
  • False or fraudulent claims are themselves a rules violation.

8. Fair play and abuse

You must be 13 or older. No bots, scripts, automated signups, mass accounts, self-referrals, referral farming, vote manipulation, exploits or interference with the service. Boosts have a 2-second cooldown and requests are rate-limited to keep play fair; referral signups from the same network are flagged for review. Accounts violating these rules may have content removed, coins/leaderboard positions reset, or be suspended.

9. Privacy

We collect the minimum needed to run the game — username, display name, emoji avatar, your email (used only to notify you about your fan page and account), a salted password hash, game data, a hashed IP for anti-abuse, and the pages/images you create. Your email is never shown publicly and is never sold. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

10. The service is provided "as is"

Grinbid is a free, hobby project provided without warranty. We don't guarantee uninterrupted service, that pages will stay online forever, or that data won't be lost (the hosting plan may reset stored data). We are not responsible or liable for user-created content, third-party images, or any disputes between fans. Use common sense and keep it kind.

11. Account deletion

Contact the admin to delete your account; your user record, transactions and page claims are removed from the active store. Pages you created are fan content and may remain (anonymously) or be removed on request.

12. Changes and contact

We may update these terms (and the game's numbers) to keep things fair and lawful; material changes will be noted in-app. Questions, claims or takedown requests: contact the Grinbid admin through the app.

🪙 Reminder: coins are virtual, free, zero cash value and non-redeemable. Pages are fan tributes, not official pages. This is a game — have fun and boost kindly.
