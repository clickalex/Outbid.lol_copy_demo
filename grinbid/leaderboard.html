Fandom first

Crown your idol 🏆

In India we don't just watch our stars — we live for them. On Grinbid the fandom leads: the most-loved celebrity or character page wears the crown. Right under them are the fans whose boosts put them there — and the top fans win coins.

💰 Real cash prizes are coming — stay tuned! Today every prize is paid in free coins. We permanently record every weekly, monthly and season winner. Once Grinbid is fully operating and legally set up, real-money prizes switch on and all winners — past and present — get paid out.

Two boards, three rounds

  • 🌟 Fandom leaderboard — the celebrity & character fan pages, ranked by total boost love (🔥). The #1 page is crowned for that round. This is shown first on the home page (top 5).
  • 🎖️ Top fans — the boosters behind them, ranked by points. The top 3 fans win the coin prizes.
  • Each board runs on three rounds at once: Weekly (7 days), Monthly (30 days) and the Season (90 days grand championship). Switch between them with the tabs on the home page.

How points & love work

  • Boost a fan page with 50+ coins → the page earns 1 love point per coin, and you earn 1 fan point per coin.
  • Boost your own fan page → ×1.5 for both the page and you.
  • Weekly, monthly and season points are tracked separately and each reset when its round settles.
  • 2-second cooldown between boosts; no bots or scripts.
  • At settlement the top 3 fans are paid coins and the top 5 fandom pages are recorded — every round, forever, in the Hall of Winners.
