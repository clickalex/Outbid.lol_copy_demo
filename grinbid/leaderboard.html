How points work

  • Boost a fan page with 50+ coins → page gets 1× points.
  • Boost your own community page → 1.5× points.
  • Your boost points also count toward your season standing.
  • 2-second cooldown between boosts.
  • Every season, points reset to 0 and a new season starts.
