🕵️ Privacy & Safety

Last updated: 30 August 2026 · We keep almost nothing, we never sell it, and we never show your email.

1. What we store

When you sign up and play, we store:

  • Your username, display name and emoji avatar (public on the leaderboard).
  • Your email address — used only to contact you about your account and fan pages (for example, when a page you submitted is approved, goes live, or needs changes). It is never shown on your profile or the leaderboard and is visible only to the site admin/moderator.
  • A salted scrypt hash of your password — never the password itself.
  • Your game data: coins, streaks, lucky-drop timestamps, task progress, boosts, referral relationships.
  • For rate-limiting and anti-bot/anti-fraud checks, a hashed fingerprint of your IP address — the raw IP is never persisted.
  • Fan pages you create: the name, category, tagline, description, tags, the photo/image you upload, your username as the page creator, and moderation status.

2. What we don't do

  • No selling, renting or sharing your data with third parties.
  • No advertising trackers, analytics pixels, fingerprinting scripts or third-party marketing.
  • No real-money payments — so there are no card or payment details to store.
  • No scraping, no phone number required, no social-account login.
  • Your email is private by design: other players never see it.

3. Uploaded photos

Photos you upload for a fan page are resized in your own browser before upload and stored solely to display on that page. Only upload images you have the right to use (your own art/photo, licensed fan art, etc.). Uploaded images are shown publicly once your page is approved. Don't upload private, explicit or copyrighted-you-don't-own images.

4. Cookies & sessions

Login uses an HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookie signed with an HMAC. It expires after 7 days (admin sessions after 2 hours). We don't use tracking cookies.

5. Referral anti-abuse review

Referral bonuses are validated for abuse: self-referrals are rejected, and signups from the same network as the referrer are flagged pending review until a moderator confirms them.

6. Data removal

Contact the admin to delete your account; your user record, transactions and page claims are removed. Fan pages you created are user content and can be removed on request. Note this is a small hobby project stored in a single data file; deleted data is removed from the active store.

7. Children

Grinbid is for users 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from under-13s; if you believe a child has an account, contact the admin and it will be removed.

8. Safety

Grinbid is a low-stakes fun game about backing faves. There is no gambling, no real money, no paid loot and no investment. If you see an abusive page, impersonation or stolen photo, report it to the admin and it will be reviewed under the Terms' takedown process.

🔒 Minimum data only: hash-only passwords, game stats, a private email for page notifications, and a hashed IP for anti-abuse. Nothing else.
