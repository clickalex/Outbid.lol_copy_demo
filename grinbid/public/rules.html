Rules of Grinbid 📜

A low-stakes, free, fan-powered carnival. Be kind, no bots, no real money.

13+ only

Virtual coins only

All coins are 100% free virtual coins with zero cash value. They are not currency, not gambling and cannot be redeemed, exchanged, refunded or sold for anything of real value.

No real-money purchases

There are no purchases, no microtransactions and no pay-to-win mechanics. No payment processor takes money for coins, boosts, ranks or perks. Any payment screen is a voluntary donation.

Fandom first — then the fans

There are two boards:

  • 🌟 Fandom leaderboard — the celebrity & character pages, ranked by the total love (🔥 boost points) their fans pour in. The most-loved page is #1 and wears the crown. This board leads the home page (top 5).
  • 🎖️ Top fans — the boosters themselves, ranked by the points they earn. The top fans win coins.

Weekly · Monthly · Season rounds

  • Both boards run on three rounds at once: Weekly (7 days), Monthly (30 days) and the Season (90-day grand championship).
  • At the end of each round, the top 3 fans win free coins — Weekly 🥇5,000 / 🥈2,500 / 🥉1,000 · Monthly 🥇20,000 / 🥈10,000 / 🥉5,000 · Season 🥇50,000 / 🥈25,000 / 🥉10,000 — and the top fandom pages are crowned and recorded.
  • Points reset for each round when it settles; every winner is recorded permanently in the Hall of Winners.

Real-money prizes — coming later

There are no real-money prizes today. Every prize is free virtual coins with zero cash value. We only switch to real cash prizes once Grinbid is fully operating and legally set up. Until then — stay tuned. When that happens, every winner already recorded (including past weekly, monthly and season winners) will be paid out. That is why we keep a permanent winners ledger. No entry fee, no purchase and no real money is ever required to play or win.

Boosts and rankings

  1. Boosts are playful virtual expressions of support.
  2. A boost costs 50+ virtual coins and adds 1× point to both the fandom page and you as a fan (1.5× on your own page).
  3. Because coins are free and non-purchasable, rankings measure fan enthusiasm — never spending power.
  4. Boosts have a 2-second cooldown to keep the feed fair.

Fan pages are user-made tributes

Every fan page is created and submitted by a real fan — there are no official pages. Pages are unofficial tributes to public figures or well-known fictional characters (actors, singers, sports stars, athletes, comic/anime/movie heroes, venues, brands). They are not affiliated with or endorsed by the real people or teams shown. The 🟢 verified badge means the real person/team (or their rep) claimed the page — not that the page is official.

Every page needs admin approval

New pages are invisible to everyone except you until an admin approves them. This keeps the board about real celebs and characters instead of spam or fakes. While pending, only you can see and self-boost your page.

Content rules — keep it a fan tribute

  • No impersonation — don't pretend to be the real person, their official team, or Grinbid staff; don't fake the verified badge.
  • Public figures & characters only — no pages for ordinary private people without their consent.
  • No hate, harassment, threats, doxxing — rival-fan banter is fine; attacking someone for who they are is not. No NSFW/explicit/sexualized content.
  • Only upload images you have the right to use — your own photo/art or licensed fan art. Don't upload stolen photos or copyrighted art. You're responsible for what you upload.
  • No spam, ads, scams, selling, or links to real-money/gambling sites.
  • Boost, don't attack — pages celebrate faves; don't make hate pages aimed at tearing someone down.

Breaking these rules can get your page rejected, taken down or your account suspended. If you're the real person (or their rep) shown on a page, use Claim page or contact the admin to get verified or request removal.

Fair play and abuse

  • You must be 13 or older.
  • No bots, scripted signups, mass accounts or spam.
  • No self-referrals. Referrals from the same IP as the referrer are flagged pending review.
  • No harassment, vote manipulation, exploits, or fraudulent claim/verification requests.
  • Accounts breaking these rules may be suspended, have content removed, or have coins/leaderboard positions reset.

Donations

Donations are voluntary contributions to keep Grinbid running. They are strictly non-reward — they never grant coins, boosts, ranks, multipliers or perks.

Changes

Numbers (streaks, drops, prizes) can be tweaked to keep the carnival fun and fair. Grinbid will never introduce real-money purchases for coins or rank advantages.

🪙 Coin reminder: today's prizes are virtual coins — free, zero cash value, no redemption. Real-money cash prizes come later after full legal setup, and all recorded winners get paid out. This is a game, not a currency.
