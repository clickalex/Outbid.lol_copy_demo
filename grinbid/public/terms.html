📜 Terms of Grinbid

Effective: today · Grinbid is a fan-made game. Play fair, be kind.

1. Virtual coins only — zero cash value

Grinbid coins are 100% free virtual coins awarded as a game mechanic (signup bonus, daily claims, lucky drops, tasks, referrals and season prizes). They hold zero cash value, are not currency, are not gambling and cannot be redeemed, exchanged, refunded or sold for anything of real value. Nothing in Grinbid is a financial product.

2. No real-money purchases

There are no purchases, no microtransactions and no pay-to-win mechanics. No payment processor (Stripe, Razorpay or otherwise) takes money for coins, boosts, ranks or perks. Any payment page you may see is a voluntary donation — see §4.

3. Boosts and rankings

Boosts are playful virtual expressions of support. A boost spends virtual coins and adds points to a fan page and to season standings. Because coins are free and non-purchasable, rankings are purely a measure of fan enthusiasm, never of spending power.

4. Donations are non-reward

Donations (UPI, PayPal, Buy Me a Coffee, Razorpay) are voluntary contributions to keep the project running. Contributors receive no coins, boosts, ranks, multipliers or perks of any kind. Donations never influence gameplay, and the app records them strictly as intent.

5. Fan-created profiles

Seeded profiles are fan-created tributes. They are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the real people, estates, venues, brands or communities they depict. Owners may submit a claim; claims are verified by moderators and marked with a verification badge.

6. Fair play and abuse

You must be 13 or older. No bots, scripted signups, self-referrals, mass accounts, harassment, spam or fraudulent claims. Referrals suspected of bot abuse are flagged and held for review. Accounts violating these rules may be suspended.

7. Changes

We may tweak the numbers (streaks, drops, prizes) to keep the carnival fun and fair. We will never introduce real-money purchases for coins or rank advantages.

🪙 Coin reminder: virtual, free, zero cash value, no redemption. This is a game, not a currency.
