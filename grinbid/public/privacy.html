🕵️ Privacy & Safety

We keep almost nothing, and we never sell it.

1. What we store

When you sign up we store: your username, display name, emoji avatar, a salted scrypt hash of your password (never the password), and your game data (coins, streaks, lucky-drop timestamps, task progress, boosts, referral relationships). For rate-limiting and anti-bot referral validation we keep a hashed fingerprint of your IP — the raw IP is never persisted.

2. What we don't do

  • No selling or sharing data with third parties.
  • No advertising trackers, analytics pixels or fingerprinting scripts.
  • No real-money payments — and therefore no payment details to store.
  • No scraping, no email required, no phone number required.

3. Sessions

Login uses an HttpOnly cookie signed with an HMAC. It expires after 7 days. You can log out at any time.

4. Referral anti-bot review

Referral bonuses are validated for abuse: self-referrals are rejected, and signups from the same IP as the referrer are flagged as pending review until a moderator confirms them. This keeps the economy fair.

5. Data removal

Contact the administrator to delete your account; your user record, transactions and profile claims are removed from the store. Donation intents contain no payment data.

6. Safety

Grinbid is a low-stakes fun game about boosting faves. There is no gambling, no real money, no loot boxes for purchase and no investment. If something feels off, that's our bug — tell us.

🔒 We collect the minimum: hash-only passwords, game stats and an IP hash for anti-abuse. Nothing else.
