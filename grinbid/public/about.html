About Grinbid 🎪

A playful, 100% free virtual-coin fan-boost game. Bid. Back. Rank up.

The idea

People love to back their faves — celebrities, influencers, estates, venues, brands and communities. Grinbid turns that enthusiasm into a fun, free leaderboard where fans boost fan-made pages and climb a weekly season. No money, no pay-to-win, no gambling.

What makes it different

🎡 Free carnival

Everything is powered by free virtual coins.

🚀 Fan-first

Every profile is a fan-made tribute, not a paid ad.

🔒 Fair by design

No purchases, no pay-to-win, no loot boxes.

🌐 Zero dependencies

Node built-ins only, vanilla JS SPA, no install.

How it's built

  • Zero-dependency Node app — node server.js with a real API, SSE live feed and JSON store.
  • Vanilla JS SPA — no framework, no build step, no install.
  • Info pages — How it works, Rules, Leaderboard, Coins, FAQ, Terms, Privacy.
