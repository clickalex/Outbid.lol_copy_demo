FAQ

Frequently asked ❓

Everything people usually ask before joining the carnival.

Is Grinbid free?

Yes. 100% free. No purchases, no microtransactions, no payment required to play.

Are coins real money?

No. Coins are virtual and have zero cash value. They cannot be purchased, sold, redeemed, exchanged or refunded.

Is this gambling or pay-to-win?

No. There is no wagering and no payment advantage. Everyone earns coins for free, so rankings measure fan enthusiasm, not spending.

How do I get started?

Sign up, collect your 2,500 coins, then boost the fan pages you love — or make a page for your fave celebrity or character yourself.

What is a boost?

A boost spends virtual coins (50+) on a fan page. It adds points to the page and to your season standing. Boosting your own page gives ×1.5 points.

What is a daily streak?

Claim once per UTC day to keep your streak. The reward starts at 500 coins and grows by 150 per streak day, capped at 2,000.

What is a lucky drop?

Every 3 hours you can open a drop worth 250–2,500 free coins. Set a reminder and keep the coins flowing.

How do referrals work?

Share your code. Your friend gets +500, you get +1,000, and you earn 10% of every coin they earn — for as long as they play.

What are the leaderboards and prizes?

There are two boards: the fandom board ranks the most-loved celebrity/character pages (shown first, top 5), and the top fans board ranks the boosters. Each runs in three rounds — weekly (7 days), monthly (30 days) and the 90-day season. At the end, the top 3 fans win free coins (weekly 5,000 / 2,500 / 1,000; monthly 20,000 / 10,000 / 5,000; season 50,000 / 25,000 / 10,000) and the top fandom pages are crowned. Every winner is recorded in the Hall of Winners.

Will there be real-money (cash) prizes?

Not yet — today every prize is free coins with zero cash value. Real cash prizes start only once Grinbid is fully operating and legally set up. We permanently record every weekly, monthly and season winner; when real money launches, all winners — including past ones — will be paid out. No entry fee or purchase is ever needed.

Are these the real celebrities' official pages?

No — every page is a fan-made tribute created by a real fan and checked by an admin before going live. If you represent the real person or their team, you can submit a claim with evidence and the page gets the 🟢 verified badge.

Can I donate?

Yes, but donations are voluntary and non-reward. They never grant coins, boosts, ranks or perks.

What do you store about me?

Your username, display name, emoji avatar, a salted password hash, game data, a hashed IP for anti-abuse, and your email. Your email is private — we only use it to tell you when your fan page is approved or needs changes.

Can I make a fan page for a real celebrity or character?

Yes — that's the whole point! Salman Khan, Hulk, Tony Stark, Dhoni, Goku: every fan page is an unofficial tribute. Tap Create, upload a photo, add details and submit. An admin reviews every new page before it goes live (to keep fakes out), and we email you when your page is approved and hits the ranking.

Why is my page not visible yet?

Fan pages go live only after admin approval. While it's pending it's private to you — you can still self-boost it at ×1.5. Approval keeps the board about real celebs and characters instead of fake pages.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Coins are always free, there is nothing to buy, and donations are voluntary with zero in-game reward. Open Grinbid and sign up to get your free 2,500 coins.
