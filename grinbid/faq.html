FAQ

Frequently asked ❓

Everything people usually ask before joining the carnival.

Is Grinbid free?

Yes. 100% free. No purchases, no microtransactions, no payment required to play.

Are coins real money?

No. Coins are virtual and have zero cash value. They cannot be purchased, sold, redeemed, exchanged or refunded.

Is this gambling or pay-to-win?

No. There is no wagering and no payment advantage. Everyone earns coins for free, so rankings measure fan enthusiasm, not spending.

How do I get started?

Sign up, collect your 2,500 coins, then boost fan pages you love or create your own community page.

What is a boost?

A boost spends virtual coins (50+) on a fan page. It adds points to the page and to your season standing. Boosting your own page gives ×1.5 points.

What is a daily streak?

Claim once per UTC day to keep your streak. The reward starts at 500 coins and grows by 150 per streak day, capped at 2,000.

What is a lucky drop?

Every 3 hours you can open a drop worth 250–2,500 free coins. Set a reminder and keep the coins flowing.

How do referrals work?

Share your code. Your friend gets +500, you get +1,000, and you earn 10% of every coin they earn — for as long as they play.

What are season prizes?

Each season runs 7 days. The top three boosters receive 50,000 / 25,000 / 10,000 free coins.

Are seeded pages real companies/people?

No. Seeded pages are fan-made tributes. Real owners can submit a claim with evidence; moderators approve and mark them verified.

Can I donate?

Yes, but donations are voluntary and non-reward. They never grant coins, boosts, ranks or perks.

What do you store about me?

Username, display name, emoji avatar, a salted password hash, game data and a hashed IP for anti-abuse. No email or phone required.

Why does the demo look like the real site?

Because it is the same app running on a mock backend in your browser. Open the demo to try it with no install.

More questions? The real app has an admin contact; in the demo, reset data with the top strip button to start fresh.
