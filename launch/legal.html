A listing is advertising space bought up front. This says exactly when the money comes back, so nobody has to argue about it.

Full refund — automatic, no explanation requested, within 5 business days

  1. We didn't publish it: 2 business days pass after a cleared payment with no live row and no written reason.
  2. The row as published isn't what you submitted — name, link, category or the amount shown — and we can't fix it to your satisfaction within one business day.
  3. We refuse the listing at screening. If the money already arrived, it goes back; nothing is charged for a listing we won't carry.
  4. Duplicate or excess payment, including a failed attempt that cleared anyway.

No refund

  1. "It didn't work." No clicks, no sales, no rankings, no coverage — we never promised an outcome (§1.2), so there is no refund for an outcome.
  2. You changed your mind about the copy, the category or your own budget after the row has been live for 7 days.
  3. You got outbid. Losing row #1 is the product functioning.
  4. You sent us wrong or incomplete information and it was published as submitted.
  5. We removed the row for breaching the rules — prohibited category, impersonation, refusing to prove domain control, abusive correspondence. No refund, and no future sales.
  6. Published prices, increments or copy change for future bids. That's not a refund event for a sold slot.

Cancellation, and how to ask

  1. You can ask us to take a row down any time; a takedown is not a cancellation, and we don't return money for placement already delivered. We will remove it anyway if being listed hurts you more than being absent.

4 · How paid placement is disclosed

The reason a site like this can exist at all: we say out loud what every row is. Applies to every row, always

  1. Every row sold for money carries, adjacent to the site name and at ≥80% of the row's own text size: “Sponsored — this position was paid for.” Not in a tooltip, not only in a footer.
  2. The board states its ranking basis next to itself: “Sorted by amount paid, highest first. Not by quality, popularity or merit.”
  3. Rows that are not paid — the free reference list of other boards — are labelled “Not a paid placement”, and any figures in that list are marked self-reported and attributed to outoutbid.lol (CC BY 4.0), because mixing paid and unpaid rows silently is how directories like this get accused of astroturfing.
  4. Amounts shown are the amounts actually paid to us, taken from the payment record. Never rounded up for looks, never "estimated", never a bidder's own claim copied into a paid column.
  5. No fabricated social proof: no invented bidder counts, no countdown that doesn't reflect a published rule, no "2 slots left" when there are ten and nobody has bought one. Words like "featured", "top rated" or "editor's pick" are never used for a bought row, because it wasn't.
  6. Paid links carry rel="sponsored nofollow". This is the machine-readable version of clause 3 and it is why we don't sell "SEO value": we'd be lying about something we can't measure.
  7. If any sentence on this site reads like a promise of results, that's a bug — tell us and we'll fix the wording the same day.
