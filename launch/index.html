How it works

Four rules, and that's the whole product.

1 · Bid

Send at least the minimum next bid for the slot you want. UPI or card link — a receipt reference is required.

2 · We publish

Within one working day your row is live with name, one-line description and link. Paid = listed, no editorial review of quality.

3 · Hold #1

Your row stays where it is until another bidder beats your total by at least 10%. Nobody is demoted by us, ever.

4 · Refund window

Wrong link, broken URL, listed in the wrong category? Fixed or refunded within 7 days. After that it's a placement, not a purchase you can return.

Why the floor is $25: at a $1 bid a card rail (2.9% + $0.30) keeps you nothing and manual UPI costs you 10 minutes of admin. Tiny bids are the fastest way to build a busy, poor board.

Rules, refunds and what we reject

    
Short on purpose. If it isn't written here, it isn't a rule. The full Terms, privacy notice, refund policy and disclosure spec are here →

    

      
What gets rejected
Anything we can't take money for: betting, real-money gaming, crypto/forex "returns", investment advice, adult, weapons, medical claims, hate, scams, clone boards that impersonate another site. We keep the list and we don't negotiate.

      
Disclosure
Every paid row shows a Sponsored mark and your total. No fake scarcity, no "almost gone", no editorial voice pretending we picked you. Paid links carry rel="sponsored nofollow" — we sell visibility, not search-engine equity, and we would rather lose a sale than promise otherwise.

      
Refunds
7 days from payment for a listing error, a dead link, a wrong category, or a rejection. Not for regret, not for "it didn't convert". Chargebacks are treated as ending the listing and the account's right to bid.

      
No viewer money
Visitors never pay, stake or earn here. No wallets, no balances, no tokens, no redeemable points. This is an advertising service: you buy a row, we publish a row.

      
Data
We keep your reply-to email, your payment reference and your published row. That's all. No trackers by default. Delete request by email and it's gone within 7 days.

      
Grievance
One address for everything, including complaints about a listing: . We answer within 72 hours.
