YOUR FLAGSHIP IDEA · VERIFIED NEW ✓

BlogRank — pay to rank, watch to earn

Bloggers & YouTubers post a link and pay to rank. Visitors click, watch or read, come back and answer a question about the content — correct answer + enough real time spent = reward. Wrong answer = nothing.

How it works
  1. Poster side — no profile needed: paste the blog/video link, pay to rank, exactly like every other pay-to-rank board.
  2. Visitor side — profile needed (to stop fraud): visitor clicks through; the click time is captured.
  3. When the visitor returns, the timer stops and the site compares time spent against the content’s real read-time / watch-time.
  4. Time is enough → answer the content question. Correct = reward paid. Incorrect = nothing.
Verification vs all 458 boards
quiz, question, answer, watch-time, read-time, proof-of-attention reward: 0 matches. Existing boards sell attention one-way; none verifies it and pays the visitor for it. Closest new board: touchinggrass.lol (listed 27 Aug) — daily verified photo from the bidder, but visitors still earn nothing.
Why it matters
Kills the #1 problem of pay-to-rank (fake/empty clicks) and adds the visitor-benefit trend your CrownRank spotted — in one product.
New — keep ✓Effort MReach ★★★★★

How to pick your one idea

Score every idea against three questions. Ship the first one that answers “yes” to at least two.

  1. One-sentence testCan you explain the site in one sentence to a stranger? “Pay to put your dog on the podium.” If it needs a paragraph, skip it.
  2. Five-dollar testCan you name a real person who would pay $5 today to be on it? Not “people” — one person. If yes, there is a market.
  3. Screenshot testWould a screenshot of the board make someone tap share? Rank walls with faces, cities, memes or pets pass; plain link lists usually fail.
